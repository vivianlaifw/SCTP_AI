# Singapore Brides 2027 and 2028: site plan

## 1. Purpose

A one-page website that invites brides-to-be in Singapore who are getting married in 2027 and 2028 to join Singapore Brides 2027 and 2028. The group is a chat group for brides-to-be to share information and get support in a community.

## 2. Visitors

- **Who:** Singaporean women aged 25 to 38 who are getting married in 2027 or 2028 and have searched for wedding-related things. Their friends and families may also open the page.
- **Device:** a phone, mostly. The page must also work on a laptop.
- **What they need:** to find out what the group is and decide whether to join. The page is meant to attract brides-to-be to join. Friends and families can see who the group is for, but only brides-to-be can join.

## 3. Page layout

One page, no menu, six sections from top to bottom.

1. **Hero (name and one line):** the banner picture across the full width. The group name, one line about the group and the join call to action start from the bottom third of the picture. The call to action links down to the sign-up section.
2. **Five reasons to join (the benefit section):** five short boxes, each with a heading and one or two lines, telling a bride-to-be why she should join.
3. **Good to know (the FAQ section):** the five questions visitors ask, each followed by its answer.
4. **Sign-up section:** a heading and one line saying sign-up opens soon.
5. **Ask section:** a heading and one line saying the chatbot is coming soon.
6. **Footer:** the group name and one short line.

## 4. Content

**Page title (browser tab):** Singapore Brides 2027 and 2028

**Section 1, hero**

- Banner picture: the watercolour image supplied by the site owner. Alternative text: "A bride in a white gown holding a bouquet and a groom in a navy suit walking away hand in hand."
- Group name: Singapore Brides 2027 and 2028
- One line: A chat group for brides-to-be to share information and get support in a community.
- Call to action, first line: Your wedding journey is easier with company.
- Call to action, second line: Join the group today!

**Section 2, five reasons to join**

The tone is professional marketing, friendly and not too formal. The reasons use only facts the site owner gave about the group. Section heading: Why join

- **Brides who are in your shoes:** Meet other brides-to-be who are getting married in 2027 or 2028, just like you.
- **Share what you know:** Swap information with other brides-to-be and get your questions answered by people who are planning too.
- **Support when you need it:** Lean on a community that is there to help you through your wedding journey.
- **No sales pitches:** The group is run as a hobby by a local wedding dress designer, with no commercials. Every conversation is about helping each other.
- **Free, and just for brides-to-be:** It costs nothing to join, and the group is only for brides-to-be, so you are among people who understand.

**Section 3, Good to know (FAQ)**

Section heading: Good to know

**Is it free to join?**
Yes, joining the group is totally free.

**Do I need to be getting married in 2027 or 2028 to join?**
Yes, the group is for brides-to-be getting married in 2027 or 2028. If you are getting married later than that, please join our 2029 brides-to-be group.

**Can my fiancé, family or friends join too?**
We are sorry, only brides-to-be can join the group, so fiancés, family and friends cannot join.

**What are the group rules, and who looks after the group?**
The group is strictly for brides-to-be to share and help each other. It is run by Vivian, a local wedding dress designer who runs her own studio, as a hobby, so there are no commercials.

**Will I receive marketing messages if I sign up?**
No, you will not receive marketing messages unless you choose to receive them.

**Section 4, sign-up**

- Heading: Sign up
- Words: Sign-up opens soon.

**Section 5, ask**

- Heading: Ask us
- Words: The chatbot is coming soon.

**Section 6, footer**

- Singapore Brides 2027 and 2028. A community for brides-to-be.

## 5. Look and feel

The colours go with the banner.

| Use | Colour | Hex code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Warm white | #FFFBF5 |
| Text | Deep navy | #2B3A55 |
| Headings | Darker navy | #1F2D4A |
| Boxes | Soft peach | #FBE9D7 |

- **Font:** an elegant serif from the fonts already on a phone or laptop: Georgia, then Times New Roman, then the browser's default serif. Body text 18px. Headings larger than body text, with the group name the largest.
- **Tone of the words:** friendly.
- **Pictures and icons:** only the banner, nothing else. The banner is a watercolour of a bride in a white gown holding a bouquet and a groom in a navy suit, walking away hand in hand, with soft peach and gold washes on a white background.
- **Readability:** text must be easy to read against its background. Navy text goes on the warm white and on the peach boxes. Where the hero text sits on the banner, it must stay easy to read; if it does not, put it directly below the picture.
- **Phone first:** it reads at 390 pixels wide with no zooming and no sideways scrolling. The banner scales down to fit the screen width.

## 6. Technical rules

- One file called index.html, with an optional style.css.
- The only other file is the banner picture, supplied by the site owner and kept in the same folder as index.html.
- Plain HTML and CSS, with no frameworks, libraries, JavaScript or build step.
- It works on Vercel with no settings.
- No keys, passwords or personal details anywhere.
- No form fields or chat box yet.
- Every file in the repository is public, this plan included.

## 7. Coming in Session 2

Neither the sign-up form nor the chatbot is built yet. Session 1 only leaves a place ready for each.

**The sign-up form will ask for:**

- **Name:** to know who is joining and to address them.
- **Wedding date:** to double-check they are eligible for the group (getting married in 2027 or 2028).
- **Email address:** so the group can reach them, and for marketing, which is sent only to people who tick the box below.
- **Phone number (optional):** same reason as the email address.
- **A tick box (optional):** "Yes, send me marketing messages." Marketing messages go only to people who tick it.

The wedding venue will not be asked. The form must say clearly that marketing messages go only to people who tick the box.

**The chatbot will answer these five questions, using the answers in Good to know:**

1. Is it free to join?
2. Do I need to be getting married in 2027 or 2028 to join?
3. Can my fiancé, family or friends join too?
4. What are the group rules, and who looks after the group?
5. Will I receive marketing messages if I sign up?

## 8. Done when

1. The layout looks nice, with no errors such as typos or misalignment.
2. The page has a hero section, a benefit section (five reasons to join), a FAQ section (Good to know), a sign-up section and a chatbot section (ask).
3. The sign-up section and the chatbot section both say they are coming soon.
4. The page answers all five questions.
5. The page reads on a phone without zooming.
6. The page has nothing the technical rules forbid.
